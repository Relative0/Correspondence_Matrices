# CM focused successor benchmark results

The bounded 108-cell RunPod screen completed with 105 successful measurements, three adapter-deadline timeouts, and no correctness mismatches.

## Exact model counting

Across 21 successful paired repetitions, d4/Ganak had a geometric-mean wall-time ratio of 0.531170 and a median ratio of 0.427506. Equivalently, d4 was 1.883x faster by geometric mean and 2.339x faster by the median paired ratio. Ganak timed out in all three repetitions of the 2,701-variable/29,534-clause case; d4 completed that case in all three repetitions.

## Closed biology fixed points

Across 30 paired repetitions, AEON/scalar had a geometric-mean ratio of 0.834794, while its median paired ratio was 2.236917. The opposing aggregate directions indicate heterogeneous model-level performance; the per-case table in RESULTS.json should be used instead of a single winner claim. All values agreed and no biology cell timed out.

## Boundaries

These are results for the predeclared focused screen, not corpus-wide claims. Timed-out pairs are excluded from paired timing ratios and reported separately. Prior affine/projected-count evidence was reused rather than rerun; projected counting remains unpaired and supports no CM speedup claim.
