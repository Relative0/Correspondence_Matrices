# Evidence-frontier research

All four requested avenues were pursued. Eight of nine original feature models
now have a checked concrete-feature projection contract; one extended format is
explicitly refused. A broader independent workload sweep completed without a
verified cross-method count or performance promotion. Exact fixture restoration
and targeted portability repairs recovered 25 of the prior 73 failing test IDs.
The maintained video compiler has an opt-in local capture adapter; no genuine
consumer session has yet been admitted.

## Broader independent projected-count workloads

Selection was fixed before timings: the three smallest complete compressed
instances, ordered by stored size then path, in each of three application
families at commit `daff1084d7487cd85f5a49f6588966275332e73f` of
[counting-benchmarks](https://github.com/dfremont/counting-benchmarks/tree/daff1084d7487cd85f5a49f6588966275332e73f).
Its projection declarations are the union of the explicit `c ind ... 0` lines.
The three selected probabilistic-model-checking instances omit that declaration;
they remain refused and were not replaced after observing outcomes.

Six information-flow and synthesis cases were measured: five methods, three
fresh-process repetitions each, eight fixed generated contexts, 30-second child
limits, 2 GiB address-space limits, and the declared bucket/CUDD bounds. Every
method ran independently of oracle success. The result is **6 complete, 54
refused, 19 timed out and 11 native errors**, across 90 scheduled cells. The
bucket methods all refused input-clause or basis-width limits. Only dynamic
CUDD completed, on the first two information-flow cases; its three repetitions
agreed internally. Those integers and timings remain unverified diagnostics.
No independent exact counting comparison completed, so no speedup is claimed.
The 11 errors preserve the native NULL-node exceptions rather than relabeling
them as measured results. All failed and timed-out cells remain in the data.

## Restored historical tests

Two portable archives contain **115 files / 143,370,966 bytes**, bound to retained
scientific manifests, including original raw records and licensed BLIFs. The
restorer checks all identities and paths before writing missing files, is
idempotent, and refuses differing existing files. A clean RunPod checkout
restored all 115 without network access. Historical hashes were not rewritten.

The source-origin audit matched 58 of 59 EPFL files to the declared upstream
commit after line-ending normalization. The retained
`best_results/size/int2float_size_2025.blif` differs; its exact historical bytes
are preserved, and the upstream-origin discrepancy remains unresolved. It is
outside the ten circuit families selected by the recovered natural-decomposition
dataset. Restored-byte identity is not a claim that every old origin declaration
was correct.

Targeted repairs make the platform-abstention test choose a genuinely different
OS, run native activation tests against the platform's own compiled library,
and bind generated chart provenance to input hashes instead of incomplete Git
history. Production routing and frozen historical binary identities are unchanged.

The final Linux replay reports **1,683 passed + 1,181
passing subtests, 30 failures, 18 errors and 3 skipped**.
All remaining failing IDs existed in the prior published Linux run. There are
zero new failing IDs; this is not a fully green historical suite or a same-host
timing comparison. The exact remaining failures and recovered IDs are in
`PUBLIC-RESULTS.json` and `VERIFICATION.json`.

## Consumer capture and next work

The recorder wraps actual `truth_layout_payload` calls during an independently
motivated video-compiler build. It records ordered request/return digests,
source and output-manifest identities, boundaries, errors, and truncation.
No arguments, output text, POP path or exception messages are stored. Digests
are not an anonymization guarantee. Operator purpose remains an attestation;
independent provenance and downstream consumption must be checked before
admission. The capture stays local. See `docs/research/CM_CONSUMER_CAPTURE.md`.

The actionable handoff is `NEXT-AGENT-PROMPT.md`. It prioritizes a real session,
the extended feature format, an independent exact control for the two completed
count cases, a new prospectively frozen bounded counting mechanism, and the
remaining historical/native source closures. Larger budgets alone do not address
the current algorithmic width refusals.

combined with the prior bound it is **$0.502547**, below the existing
$10 authorization. Nonrefunded new reservations total $1.75.
