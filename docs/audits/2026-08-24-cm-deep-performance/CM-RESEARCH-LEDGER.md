## Applicability summary

- **Implement now:** conservative width selector and immutable derived-metadata memoization. These are supported by local paired measurements and preserve the exact artifact.
- **Prototype after a trace exists:** incremental tracked queries, byte-aware/content-addressed persistence, compact interning keys, JIT/native fused word kernels.
- **Use as task-matched alternatives:** BDD restriction for many contexts; IPASIR for repeated SAT queries.
- **Reject for current complete-output contract:** general e-graph replacement, sparse matrices, arbitrary tensor/Kronecker decomposition, direct bigint JIT, GPU/distributed execution without a larger measured workload.

Research-source performance claims were not transplanted into CM. Every applicability decision above is an audit inference tied to CM’s artifact and the local profile.
