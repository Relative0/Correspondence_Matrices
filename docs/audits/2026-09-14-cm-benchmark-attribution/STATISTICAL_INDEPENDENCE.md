The existing biology and exact-count timings establish comparator behavior, not
a CM-specific benefit. None of the material findings in
`CLAIM_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.json` qualifies as an attribution-isolated CM effect.
Generic packed elimination, factor arithmetic, masks, caching, streaming and
indexed validation remain useful engineering results with narrower wording.
This addendum preserves the original reports, raw bytes, names and gates.

## Units and estimands

A run is a repeated measurement on one input. A perturbation is a related query
within one model session. A renamed variable basis, revised model, conditioned
slice, width variant, or alternative translation is not automatically a new
independent dataset. Neither 108 screen cells nor 117,504 native output checks
are counts of independent scientific units.

The analysis uses candidate time divided by baseline time; below one favors the
candidate. Within a case/session size it geometrically averages paired repeat
ratios. Within a family it geometrically averages those case estimates. The
family-weighted summary then gives equal weight to families. Case-weighted
geometric means, median case ratios, geometric means of ratios of arm medians,
and ratios of summed case median times are also retained. These estimate
different objectives; none can replace another without a declared weighting
rule. Repetition counts never increase a family's weight.

Bootstrap intervals use 10,000 fixed-seed draws of whole cluster estimates, with
linear-interpolated 2.5th/97.5th percentiles. They are descriptive resampling
intervals for an exposed convenience cohort. They do not demonstrate nominal
95% population coverage, resolve unrecorded ancestry, or create held-out data.
Intervals are withheld for fewer than two clusters and explicitly flagged as
fragile below five. Leave-one-cluster-out ranges expose influential families.
No pooled-repetition interval is presented as dataset uncertainty. Within-case
repeat ranges describe measured timing variability without implying p99 tails.

The biology clusters come from `BIOLOGY_CORPUS_AUDIT.json`: shared named ancestry
and syntax relationships conservatively join variants. The ten successor models
yield nine clusters because the A/B asymmetric-cell-division models share a
family. Distinct identifiers alone are not proof of statistical independence;
these clusters are provisional controls against known dependence, not a random
sample of biology. The full corpus audit records further ancestry candidates.

The retained `input-freeze-009/INPUT_FREEZE.json` maps every exact-count input
SHA-256 to the pinned counting-benchmarks source. All eight successor cases come
from **cachet-plan-recognition**: log-1/2/4/5, tire-2/3, and 4step/5step.
Consequently the primary conservative summary treats that shared application
family as one cluster. Three named subfamilies (log, tire, step) supply a separate
sensitivity; their independent generation has not been established. This is a
concrete coverage limit, not broad counting-family diversity. A separate
instance-as-cluster sensitivity is explicitly optimistic and cannot support a
population confidence claim. Historical synthetic
mask/factor/bucket widths are collapsed to generator families; GNU Radio LDPC
inputs are collapsed to their shared source family; native batch variants use
the two structural generators, balanced tree and layered sharing. Distinct
public bucket model systems are separate provisional clusters. This may
over-cluster some data, but prevents manufactured precision from related inputs.

## Censoring and correctness denominators

The 2,701-variable, 29,534-clause case has three completed d4 runs and three
Ganak adapter-deadline timeouts near 60 seconds. Ganak completion time is
right-censored. The script records deadline, observed parent time and each
d4 time. Dividing d4 time by the 60-second budget is reported only as a threshold
sensitivity; 60 seconds is not substituted for a completed Ganak runtime.
The missing pair never becomes zero, infinity, an invented finite time or an
extra victory in the paired timing estimator. Completion rate and reason remain
separate outcomes. Conditional timing summaries cannot establish an
unconditional expected runtime for the corpus.

Zero count mismatches means zero contradictions among available successful
results. On this censored case, repeated d4 values show internal consistency;
they do not supply an independent Ganak agreement. The original screen's frozen
`worker_error` rows keep their original classification and separate posthoc
admission/abort/deadline interpretation. Refusals and native errors are not
right-censored timing samples and never become measured answers.

## Historical uncertainty and mechanism limits

The reanalysis preserves the historical lifecycle boundaries. Resident cold
totals, warm queries, full process lifetime, traced Python allocations and OS
high-water memory are distinct. q8 restrictions often lower live width relative
to unrestricted q1; that comparison is not a pure batch-scaling experiment.
Changing native order, host or timeout does not create a paired comparison.

The native batch's frozen estimator and 1.10x scalar/batch requirement remain
authoritative for its old disposition: reported q96 1.0781x failed. Our paired
log-ratio summaries and ratios-of-medians sensitivities use explicitly different
estimators. They cannot reopen the gate, remove cleanup, or retroactively apply
the less demanding new 5% total-cost rule. The two synthetic generator clusters
also do not supply broad application confidence.

Historical paired bootstrap intervals mainly describe repeatability on fixed
inputs. Large factors versus CSE or dense FLINT are useful explanations of
avoided expansion/conversion; stronger CUDD and M4RI controls materially limit
the inference. Matched indexed-validation and array-backend ablations isolate
general mechanisms. They do not isolate a uniquely CM representation effect.
The register includes unfavorable cases, refused work, consumed follow-ups,
semantics corrections and the absence of a natural backend-consumer trace.

## Prospective held-out gate

The next experiment must freeze the family assignment, q1/q8/q64 perturbation
traces, exact outputs, algorithm/backend controls, lifecycle and primary
aggregation rule before held-out timing. All previously measured models and
their relatives belong in development/regression; an untouched model file is
not held out if its ancestry cluster has already influenced the candidate.
The remaining closed corpus can support correctness coverage even where too
few independent unseen families remain for a reliable performance gate.

For a predeclared primary CM-prepared versus same-algorithm raw-ingress contrast,
accept only if the held-out family-weighted total-session cost ratio is at most
0.95 and the upper bound of a meaningful two-sided 95% cluster-aware interval is
strictly below 1.0, with exact outputs/witnesses and no hidden failures. Charge
raw parsing, CM construction/preparation, query binding, all q queries, required
delivery and cleanup according to the frozen session contract. Warm-only wins
do not pass a total-cost gate. An explicitly packed CM arm must use actual CM
representation and disclose feasibility limits.

Declare one primary session size/contrast or a multiplicity-controlled decision
rule; do not search q1/q8/q64 and backends for a favorable unadjusted interval.
Use paired counterbalanced repeat blocks for precision within models, but
resample whole independent families for population-oriented uncertainty.
Predeclare the minimum informative family count and interval method using
development-only simulations or precision analysis; a minimum count alone does
not guarantee validity. If ancestry, representativeness, censoring, correctness,
or independent cluster count makes the interval uninterpretable, record
`insufficient evidence` and do not pass the gate. Wider uncertainty is a valid
finding. Extra repetitions cannot cure missing independent families.

Only a controlled difference between raw and CM-prepared ingress with the same
factorized algorithm, primitives, cache lifetime, exact outputs and backend can
attribute an observed difference to the CM preparation/representation package.
Further ablations are needed to split normalization, representation and reuse.
AEON, scalar oracle and native SAT/counting stay essential independent controls,
but their relative timing alone cannot identify that CM contribution.
