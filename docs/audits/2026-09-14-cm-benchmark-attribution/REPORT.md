## Uncertainty and applicability

`STATISTICAL_REANALYSIS.json` reconstructs per-instance repeat ratios, failure records, cluster summaries and sensitivity across 47 evidence sources. Repeated runs are measurement repetitions, not independent datasets. In biology, AEON/oracle equal-instance geometric ratio is 0.834794, but the median instance geometric ratio is 2.378708 and the family-weighted descriptive 95% bootstrap interval is approximately [0.1221, 3.4325]. Different aggregation objectives give opposing conclusions; a single winner claim is not defensible.

All eight successor exact-count instances come from the same cachet plan-recognition family. The successful-pair d4/Ganak geometric ratio 0.531170 excludes the eighth, censored case. The primary one-source-family population interval is withheld. Even a more permissive log/tire/step subfamily sensitivity spans parity, approximately [0.2902, 1.2866]. An apparently favorable interval obtained by treating every file as independent cannot substitute for verified ancestry. The three Ganak timeouts remain explicit right-censored evidence; d4-only completion is not paired count agreement for that case.

No historical result passes the new CM-specific heldout gate merely through reanalysis. That gate requires at least 5% lower total-session cost and a two-sided 95% cluster interval wholly below parity on an eligible frozen held-out population, with matched task/lifecycle contracts and attribution ablations. Descriptive resampling of an exposed convenience cohort does not establish that condition.
