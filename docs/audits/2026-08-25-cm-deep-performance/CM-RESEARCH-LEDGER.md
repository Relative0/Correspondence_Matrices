## Applicability conclusions

- **Implemented from local evidence:** remove the redundant id-keyed memo only on the sharing-aware builder path; preserve it on the legacy path.
- **Still plausible but trace-gated:** byte/cost-aware cache policy, dependency-query incremental compilation, family/version change-impact analysis, BDD manager reuse, and feature-based backend selection.
- **Still plausible but dependency/hardware-gated:** fused preallocated native/JIT `uint64` kernels for genuine repeated batches, with AVX2 baseline and optional AVX-512 ternary fusion.
- **Conditional mathematical opportunities:** independent variable-block decomposition and lazy layout/permutation views can reduce avoidable intermediates, but not the final exact-output lower bound.
- **Rejected for the current workload:** broad e-graph replacement, general sparse/numerical tensor methods, direct bigint JIT, default multiprocessing/GPU/distributed execution, and support-only selector retuning.

No external source’s speedup is treated as CM evidence. Every verdict is tied to the current code, complete-output contract, and local/accepted profiling.
