# Documentation and media notation

- In mathematical displays for CM papers, scripts, captions, storyboards, and videos, write logical exclusive-or with Unicode `⇕` (U+21D5) or LaTeX `\Updownarrow`.
- Do not use `⊕` or LaTeX `\oplus` for logical exclusive-or. Prose and narration may say “exclusive-or” or “XOR” when that is clearer than speaking the symbol.
- Apply this rule to new work and successor versions. Preserve frozen historical artifacts and correct them through a new version rather than editing them in place.
- The detailed project convention is recorded in `CM_NOTATION_STANDARD_V1.md`.
- For future work, follow the additive updates in `CM_NOTATION_STANDARD_V3.md`
  (which incorporates v2). Implication between expressions uses the short double
  arrow `⇒`, requested as LaTeX `\Implies`, as does its bracketed CM `[⇒]`.
  Do not use a single right arrow for implication or mathematical inference.
  Use `=` for equal expressions; distinguish diagram motion from logical claims.
  This newer rule also applies within `video_factory`; preserve its frozen v2
  conventions and delivery files rather than rewriting historical versions.
