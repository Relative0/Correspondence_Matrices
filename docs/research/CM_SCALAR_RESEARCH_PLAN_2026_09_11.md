## Questions and controls

1. Can conservative support decomposition count OR, XOR and mixed expressions efficiently, including overlapping subgroups and genuinely inseparable negative controls? Implement a separate exact count plan with integer zero/one arithmetic, packed fallback and eager width guards. Compare against CSE packed evaluation, the existing AND-only plan, and native CUDD with natural, grouped and dynamic variable orders. All costs include document decode, conversion, plan construction and query delivery; prepared reuse is a separate measurement. No claim of a new general knowledge compiler.
2. Can packed GF(2) elimination count affine constraints usefully on public parity-check matrices? Compare against native FLINT dense conversion, construction, rank and augmented rank. Accept exact integer answers only. Test expression and CM ingress separately; reject nonlinear constraints. This is an application-motivated derived codeword/partial-assignment count, not a decoder benchmark or evidence of a production workload.
