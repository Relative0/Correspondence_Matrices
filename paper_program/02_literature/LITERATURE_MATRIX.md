## Novelty boundary after the audit

The following are antecedents or standard consequences and should not be claimed as new: storing a truth table in a matrix, treating Boolean connectives as matrix operators in general, XOR-AND Boolean matrix multiplication, uniqueness under a fixed ordering, all 16 binary connectives, Kronecker/minterm evaluation, pointwise truth-table fusion, basic input/output symmetries, basis expansion of the Boolean function space, support inclusion, and matrix representations of multi-input Boolean functions.

The candidate new contribution is narrower: a typed correspondence-matrix operator representation in which row/column transformations can provide signed operand alignment before sound same-operands fusion, together with a reproducible study of when that rewrite reduces actual compiled work after all costs are included. The current pair compiler implements exact-pair fusion but not this broader alignment pass. A formula-valued LM lift and valuation-commutation theorem may be a secondary formal contribution, but the outer-product and operator-basis ingredients are antecedented by Boolean matrix theory and matrix logic. The individual algebraic identities are elementary or antecedented; publishability must come from the integrated method, correctness argument, and honest empirical boundary.

## Collective difference map

| Feature | Mizraji vector logic | Cheng/Zhao STP and Boolean matrix work | Eigenlogic | Current CM/LM formulation | Defensible conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scalar algebra | Ordinary real matrix algebra on orthonormal truth vectors | Semi-tensor products and, in the Boolean-calculus paper, explicit XOR-AND products | Real/complex operator and projector language | XOR-AND contraction, equivalently `GF(2)` matrix contraction for binary entries | The CM algebra differs from Mizraji but overlaps Cheng directly. |
| Binary operator shape | Typically maps a Kronecker-product input space to an output truth vector | A 2 x 4 logical structure matrix for a two-input Boolean output, with encoding conventions | Projectors/observables on tensor spaces | A 2 x 2 scalar truth operator with X on rows and Y on columns | The 2 x 2 layout is a compact reshape/typing choice, not new truth information. |
| Polynomial relation | Not the central representation | ANF polynomial coefficients are constructed in a separate coordinate system | Polynomial form is not the central distinction | LM entries are formulas over operand polarities; valuation yields a numeric CM | LMs are not Zhao-Gao-Cheng ANF coefficient objects, and CM entries are truth values rather than ANF coefficients. |
| Linearity | Linear maps over the chosen vector space | Linear Boolean functions and invariant/variant linear structures, plus matrix representations | Linear-operator formalism | Evaluation is linear in CM coefficients over `GF(2)` for fixed operands | The manuscript must name the domain and avoid treating these as the same theorem. |
| Bra-ket evaluation | Operator application to truth vectors; Stern also uses outer-product state bases | Usually delta-vector/STP notation | Genuine projector/eigenvalue and probability interpretation | XOR-AND bra-CM-ket selection; formula-valued bra-LM-ket pairing | The notation and outer-product basis are antecedented; the LM pairing is relational, not quantum measurement. |
| Transformations | Negation and operator identities, including tensor constructions | Swap and negation matrices | Complement/projector transformations | Transpose and row/column permutations used to normalize operand frames | The transformations are standard; their compiler role may be distinctive. |
| Operator combination | Logical operator identities can be derived algebraically | Pointwise truth-table combination is explicit prior art | Operators combine within the projector framework | Normalize, combine operator tokens, and evaluate the fused token | The combination identity is antecedented; the normalization-plus-fusion algorithm and cost boundary are the candidate contribution. |
