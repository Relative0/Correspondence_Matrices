# Correspondence Matrices claim and proof ledger

Audit date: 2026-09-14  
Prospective author: **Brian Theory**

Status key: **retain** = usable after normal editing; **repair** = central but presently misstated or incomplete; **test** = plausible empirical claim needing stronger evidence; **withdraw** = false, unsupported, or strategically harmful; **reserve** = omit from the first paper unless later work makes it independently defensible.

This ledger separates mathematical truth from novelty and usefulness. A result can be correct yet already standard, and a valid identity can still fail to provide a computational advantage after representation and preprocessing costs are counted.
