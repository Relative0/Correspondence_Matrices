An independent verifier checks counts, hashes, truth digests and median arithmetic.

## Later natural-circuit correctness and timing addendum, 26 September 2026

The separate [natural extension](../04_NATURAL_CIRCUIT_EXTENSION_20260926/README.md) screened 2,861 original EPFL primary outputs under rules fixed before CM compilation, admitting 71 from ten designs. The pinned ABC executable converted all 71 to truth-equivalent AIGs. A frozen correctness run passed all 71 cases against the original BLIF with direct packed AST, ordinary CM IR, ABC AIG, and every returned pair token. Under `hybrid`, four selected roots were pure structural, one was fully retabulated, and 66 fell back. The subsequent [frozen descriptive secondary timing run](../05_NATURAL_TIMING_20260926/README.md) completed paired Python producer timing for 70 cases, with one retained calibration failure. Its case-median direct/CM ratio is 0.0376, and direct packed evaluation was faster on all 70 complete cases. ABC conversion and exhaustive AIG truth checking completed 15 repetitions on all 71 cases, but ABC subprocess wall time is not a matched ratio against resident Python producer times. This does not alter the failed v4 primary result or resolve P14 historical runtime identity. ROBDD/generic comparisons, sharing/reuse/output regimes, independent-family prevalence, and a full matched systems campaign remain open.
