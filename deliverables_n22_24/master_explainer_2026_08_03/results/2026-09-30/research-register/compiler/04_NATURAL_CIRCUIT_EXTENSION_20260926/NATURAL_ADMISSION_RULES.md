# Prospective natural-circuit admission rules

Date: 2026-09-26. Status: fixed **before any CM pair compilation or natural-case timing**. This is an admission freeze, not an execution freeze or a completed protocol-v3 circuit result. The synthetic v4 primary result remains negative and cannot be replaced by this secondary arm.

Source: the 20 original combinational BLIFs in `arithmetic/` and `random_control/` of `lsils/benchmarks` commit `0060e156826e733d69bf5b3322d1bdd0d03a1f9a`. All 20, including families examined in prior P1–P14 work, enter the screening denominator. This is an EPFL-structure cohort, not an independent previously uninspected-family holdout. The copied original files and MIT notice are in `epfl_original/`; their bytes must match the pinned Git blobs.

One source case is one **original primary output cone**; internal signals are not substituted to improve eligibility. The strict parser accepts one `.model`, `.inputs`, `.outputs`, `.names`, and `.end`, at most 16 MB, 4096 inputs/outputs, 500,000 nodes, six fanins and 64 same-polarity cubes per LUT. Rejected designs and output cones remain recorded. Relevant fanins are those constrained by at least one cube, and support is the transitive set of primary inputs through those fanins.

An output is structurally eligible exactly when it is driven by a `.names` node, has support 2–12, at most 128 distinct source nodes, and source depth at most 128. Within each design, rank eligible output names by SHA-256 of UTF-8 `EPFL-v5:` + design name + `:` + output name, ascending, breaking ties by output name; take the first eight without replacement. Do not backfill a selected output that fails translation. The translator preserves each BLIF node's fanin and cube order, builds balanced ANDs within cubes and balanced ORs across cubes, memoizes by source signal, and represents constants using the first support variable. It has a 4096-unique-AST-node cap. Record original file and Git-blob hashes, source size, support, selected output, translated DAG hash, AST occurrences and unique identities, sharing and equal-but-distinct counts, and an exhaustive source-BLIF truth digest. Group equal truth digests at equal support width without dropping cases.

Screening and translation use no CM compiler and no timing. The execution freeze must subsequently fix ABC binary SHA-256, version/build, libraries, supported syntax, exact import/strash/optimization/query commands, cone export, timeouts, generated plus natural cases, correctness checks, analysis, environment and initially empty result files. The ABC arm is mandatory for the translatable subset. Until that freeze passes, all natural-circuit performance and prevalence findings are **unmeasured**. Missing ABC or an unsupported BLIF case cannot be silently replaced by a favorable subset.
