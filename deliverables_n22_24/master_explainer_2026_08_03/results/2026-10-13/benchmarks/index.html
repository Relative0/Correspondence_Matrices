Exploratory CPU run: diagnostic only

Two regression inputs: onehot BLIF and s27 CNF. No writable delegated cgroup child, so the strict kernel memory requirement was unmet. Supervisor wall had an approximately 125 ms common floor. Worker phases are diagnostics: CM IR was slower than CSE; Bucket preparation was faster than CUDD, while CUDD prepared queries were faster. Completion and evidence boundaries

These schedules are complete. Broader workload and host qualification, projected-count confirmation, and the 201 native CUDD tests excluded from the separate hardening integration remain outside this publication.

Both local confirmations used owned cgroup-v2 limits with verified cleanup. The direct schedule had 24 valid cells and 258 oracle output checks; the Bucket schedule had 22 cells and 180 prepared queries. The exploratory schedule had 44 cells and 360 prepared queries.
