Keep explicit backend selection. All 112 cells and 56 pairs passed independent full-output verification. No candidate had a prepared full-task paired median below 1; these cases do not justify a dispatch threshold or a general CM speedup.

What the phases show

CSE lowers the small integer query subtotal but its preparation cost remains charged. CM adds compilation cost with similar q64 binding/query totals. NumPy words does not consistently offset mask and conversion preparation. Validation and process/import costs dominate these short full-task lifecycles. A resident-process application with a different validation contract needs its own measurement; these values cannot be substituted for that task.

Resources and recovery

Conservative elapsed/core accounting, including offline restoration and failed startups: 473.05 seconds, below both 1,200-second caps. Experiment cgroup peak: 60.58 MiB; the enclosing service also enforced one CPU, 2 GiB memory and zero swap. This experiment peak excludes guardian/restoration processes outside that branch. No experiment limit events, malformed output, timeout or incomplete pair occurred. Offline restoration matched all 2,056 frozen runtime hashes before timing. No paid resources or optional native installs were used.
