## Executive verdict

F1, F2, F3, and Regime B of F5 survive fresh measurement, with narrower wording.
F4's end-to-end measurements survive but its representation-mechanism interpretation
does not. F5 Regime A is **refuted as labeled**: the generator did not guarantee

third-party record intact, but the external memory note had inherited the F4/F5
overclaims and required an Audit V3 addendum.

The largest correction is F5. The alleged “all-variables-live” generator permits

```text
Xor(Xor(a, b), Eqv(a, Not(b)))
```

and `Eqv(a, Not(b)) == Xor(a, b)`, so the result is constant false. Its retry loop tests
`len(CMNode.vars) == n`, which is post-rewrite syntactic support, not semantic influence.
Exact BDD support shows only **4 of 29** committed rows were truly all-live; median
semantic support was 16, and the published n=32 formula had support 16.

### Updated honest headline

- The words engine is bit-exact and moves the shared full-output cost floor by roughly
  6–8× at n=24 on this machine; both CM and raw Bitset receive it, although their
  realized speedups are not numerically identical.
- On shallow sparse formulas, the end-to-end CM wrapper is at parity to a modest Bitset
  disadvantage. Repeated 300-formula n=24 runs span **1.02–1.09 CM/Bitset**, not one
  universal 1.02.
- The archived wrapper series crosses to CM at larger ambient n, but same-formula
  isolation attributes essentially all n-dependent drift to fixed-binding key work in
  the current matched-scope control, not an inherently worsening Bitset kernel.
- On a corrected, exactly all-live sharing-rich family, CM is **1.75–2.52× faster
  through n=26**. This remains a favorable upper bracket; there is no certified n=32
  all-live result.
- Beyond-guard formulas remain exactly computable over the retained scope at near
  parity (archived median 0.96; six-row local V3 median 0.95). The guard is a policy cap,
  not a kernel capability wall.

## F1 — words backend

**Verdict: CONFIRMED, with one wording correction.**

### Static proof

`_compute_word_plan` resolves all input locations and chooses the output buffer before
processing `release_after[op_index]`. Therefore the free list contains only buffers from
earlier operations; no current input buffer can be selected as the current output.
Inputs that die at this operation are recycled only after the operation completes.

- Repeated operands are counted once per occurrence by `_last_use_releases`; the
  remaining count reaches zero exactly once, so they cannot double-release.
- Variadic AND/OR/XOR retains every current input until all in-place reduction steps
  complete.
- IMP and EQV retain both inputs until their two in-place numpy steps complete.
- The plan depends only on program topology. Width changes replace only scratch arrays,
  not locations, so cache eviction cannot change aliasing.

### Fresh measurement

The original verifier was rerun on both interpreters:

| Interpreter | Checks | n=24 CM speedup | n=24 raw speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 3.10.11 | 1,827/1,827 | 7.13× | 8.21× |
| 3.13.5 | 1,827/1,827 | 6.28× | 7.47× |

Both reproduce the crossover: words loses at n=12, is near crossover at n=14, wins from
n=16, and is in the claimed ~7× class by n=24.

An additional 79 checks per interpreter covered repeated operands, variadic operations,
IMP/EQV fanout, all-fixed actual variables, exact n=5 fallback versus n=6 words dispatch,
seven environment keys against the four-entry cache, and three widths against the
two-width scratch cache. All passed by complete packed equality.

The symmetric-mechanism fairness invariant is confirmed: both sides use the same words
engine. The statement “the CM/Bitset ratio is unchanged” is too strong; fresh n=24
realized gains differ by 15–19%.

## F2 — threshold 16

**Verdict: CONFIRMED and adopted as the harness default.**

The archived scripts use deterministic paired populations, warm both paths, select a
per-formula repetition count before the five timed rounds, alternate order, take a
within-formula median, then report the median across formula ratios. Repetition selection
is based on CM time but the same count is used for both sides; it changes precision, not
the ratio target.

Audit V3 timed thresholds 7 and 16 plus raw Bitset together in a rotating order on the
same 1,500 formulas. All complete outputs were bit-identical.

| n | live_k | count | threshold 16 / threshold 7 | threshold 16 / Bitset |
|---:|:---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | 8–11 | 44 | 0.113 | 1.124 |
| 18 | 8–11 | 47 | 0.031 | 1.342 |
| 20 | 8–11 | 68 | 0.031 | 1.377 |
| 22 | 8–11 | 61 | 0.029 | 1.264 |
| 24 | 8–11 | 59 | 0.033 | 1.293 |

For live_k≤7, both thresholds execute the same branch; paired medians stayed within
about ±6%, with no systematic threshold-16 regression. The n=16, live_k 8–11 stratum
also improves about 8.9× despite full 2^16 output.

Changed:

- `BenchmarkConfig.cm_hybrid_threshold`: 7 → 16;
- `config_from_args` fallback: 7 → 16;
- CLI `--cm-hybrid-threshold` default: 7 → 16.

The lower-level `cm_ir.py` library defaults remain 7.

Artifacts: `CM_V3AUDIT_F2_threshold_paired_{raw,summary}.csv`.

## F3 — sampling-luck correction

**Verdict: CONFIRMED in direction; PARTIAL on the exact 1.02 constant.**

The 300-formula methodology is statistically reasonable for a typical-formula ratio:
paired formulas, alternating method order, adaptive repetitions selected before timed
rounds, five within-formula rounds, and a median across per-formula ratios. It does not
estimate a ratio of aggregate total runtime, and microsecond-scale session effects remain.

Results:

- archived threshold-16 n=24 population: 1.016;
- Audit V3, same population in a three-way interleaved run: 1.053;
- fresh seed population 13,700,000 (300 formulas): 1.089;
- fresh seed population 28,900,000 (300 formulas): 1.079.

All 600 fresh-seed outputs were complete packed matches. Thus the 8-formula 0.84 result
was sampling luck, but “the robust value is 1.02” is session-specific. Public pages now
say 1.02–1.09. No page uses 0.84 at n=24 as a current result; it appears only in the
transparent correction narrative.

Artifacts: `CM_V3AUDIT_F3_n24_seeds_{raw,summary}.csv`.

## F4 — ambient-n wrapper drift

**Verdict: MEASUREMENTS CONFIRMED; STATED REPRESENTATION MECHANISM REFUTED.**

The archived depth-4 series is internally consistent: CM remains near 3.3 µs while the
matched-scope raw control rises from 3.34 to 4.02 µs between n=24 and n=32.

Audit V3 held the formula, support, FlatProgram, and output width fixed, changing only
ambient n and the number of irrelevant fixed-map entries:

| Component median | n=24 | n=32 | delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| full raw control | 7.08 µs | 8.91 µs | +1.83 µs |
| `_bind_flat_program` cache hit | 3.37 µs | 4.87 µs | +1.50 µs |
| prebound operation evaluation | 2.42 µs | 2.35 µs | -0.08 µs |
| CM wrapper | 7.85 µs | 7.73 µs | -0.13 µs |

The binder delta alone is 82% of the full raw delta; the prebound kernel has no positive
drift. Within measurement noise, all isolated n-dependence is fixed-key
sorting/construction/hash/cache lookup. This is a genuine cost of the current end-to-end
matched-scope API, but not an inherent O(n) cost of Bitset evaluation and not evidence
that CM's representation advantage grows across these ambient sizes.

Artifacts: `CM_V3AUDIT_F4_binding_profile_{raw,summary}.csv`.

## F5 — comprehensive campaign

**Verdict: Regime A REFUTED AS LABELED; sharing bracket PARTIAL; Regime B CONFIRMED.**

### Generator and liveness

The retry loop guarantees only `len(compile_expr_to_cm_ir(expr).vars) == n`.
`CMNode.vars` is a sound post-rewrite support over-approximation; it is not exact
semantic support. Exact BDD influence over all committed seeds found:

- 29 full-output rows total, not 30;
- 4/29 semantically all-live;
- median semantic live_k = 16;
- two n=18 formulas are constants;
- n=32 trial 0 has semantic live_k = 16.

The n=32 CSV row is arithmetically consistent (3.8556 s / 7.7780 s, a 2.02× CM
advantage; estimated 48.53 GB under a recorded 128 GB limit; equality and sampled-oracle
flags true). It remains a valid full-*ambient-output* timing, but it cannot certify
all-variable liveness. No paid big-pod rerun was justified after the premise failed.

### Sharing-rich bracket

The family still measures deliberate sharing. Across its 29 rows:

- median raw-AST ops / CM ops = 4.35;
- median CM/Bitset = 0.538;
- correlation between compression and Bitset/CM advantage = 0.945
  (log/log correlation 0.927).

Sparse depth-4 formulas have median compression 1.12 and median CM/Bitset 1.12. The
upper/lower family framing is quantitatively supported as a sharing bracket, but the old
“all-live through n=32” framing is not.

Audit V3 generated a corrected family by removing the cancelling EQV mixer and checking
exact BDD support before timing. All 42 formulas through n=26 were exactly all-live and
complete packed CM/raw matches:

| n | trials | raw ops / CM ops | CM/Bitset | CM advantage |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | 10 | 3.13 | 0.476 | 2.10× |
| 18 | 10 | 4.35 | 0.526 | 1.90× |
| 20 | 8 | 4.11 | 0.573 | 1.75× |
| 22 | 6 | 5.34 | 0.481 | 2.08× |
| 24 | 5 | 6.73 | 0.397 | 2.52× |
| 26 | 3 | 3.87 | 0.527 | 1.90× |

### Beyond guard

Six locally regenerated rows—one high-live_k example from every n/depth cell, including
retained_k 24, 25, and 26—were complete packed matches and passed 2,000 scalar samples
each. Local CM/Bitset median was 0.949 (range 0.841–0.994), consistent in direction with
the archived 0.96. “True support” is corrected to “retained scope”; minimal semantic
support was not established.
