## 3. Product outcome

Build a local-first, modular Python system that can learn and evaluate at least
these distinct capabilities:

1. **Task-aware algorithm routing** — predict the fastest safe exact backend for
   a requested output and resource budget.
2. **Computational-subclass recognition** — identify candidates such as affine
   XOR systems, independent components, low-width structure, repeated cofactors,
   Horn/dual-Horn/2-CNF fragments, mux/ITE trees, arithmetic blocks, cardinality
   constraints, or other structures with specialized exact algorithms.
3. **Verified semantic macro recognition** — propose that a large or structurally
   obscured subgraph implements a reusable function or generalized Boolean
   identity, then prove or independently verify it before replacement.
4. **Rewrite profitability and scheduling** — rank regions, rule directions,
   sequences, and stopping points while using only admitted sound rewrites.
5. **Representation, partition, layout, and ordering selection** — rank CM layouts,
   BDD variable orders, decompositions, AIG cuts, or compilation targets.
6. **Exact-search guidance** — optionally provide one-shot or bounded advice for
   SAT, equivalence-miter, or exact model-counting search while leaving the solver
   complete and independently checkable.
7. **Novelty, confidence, and abstention** — detect distribution shift and fall
   back to a deterministic policy when predicted benefit or safety is uncertain.
8. **Generalized rule or macro discovery** — offline only, infer parameterized
   identities or abstractions from corpora, prove them, and compile approved rules
   into a cheap deterministic matcher.

Do not require one model to perform all eight tasks. Separate models and exact
detectors are preferred when their objectives, features, costs, or verification
contracts differ.

### Research priors to revalidate, not inherited facts

Owner-supplied research preceding this prompt found these provisional directions:

- a historical bigint/word crossover rule made many costly choices, but a newer
  conservative `k >= 16` rule appeared to reduce mean regret to roughly 1% on its
  measured corpus;
- therefore a learned bigint-versus-words selector by itself may have too little
  headroom to repay inference cost, while a broader task-aware portfolio may not;
- complete truth-vector computation remained output-bound;
- CM preparation, especially interning and related compiler work, appeared more
  important than a residual CM-versus-flat kernel difference;
- related-family and partial-context reuse helped CM relative to uncached CM but
  did not beat the strongest measured task-matched baselines; and
- equality saturation, deep models, JIT, native SIMD, GPU, and new cache policies
  were not justified for the then-current isolated exact-output workload without
  a new oracle gap or real workload.

These observations may depend on dirty/uncommitted product work and are not an
authorized dataset or accepted baseline. Reproduce them from an owner-approved
immutable base and artifact snapshot before using them as labels or public claims.
Do not tune a model only to rediscover the `k >= 16` rule.

## 4. Define the requested computation before choosing a method

Model the query as a first-class, versioned contract. At minimum distinguish:

- complete packed or dense truth-vector evaluation;
- one assignment evaluation;
- satisfiability and one satisfying assignment;
- validity;
- exact equivalence or implication;
- exact model count;
- restriction/cofactor under partial assignments;
- repeated related-expression or version queries;
- simplification/minimization under a stated cost function;
- semantic difference or change-impact analysis;
- CM-native transformation artifacts, including directional quotient or feature
  artifacts that are not semantic XOR;
- recognition of higher-level functional blocks; and
- production of a compact symbolic representation rather than explicit output.

Never compare unlike artifacts as one speed contest. SAT, BDD construction,
truth-vector extraction, CM quotient, symbolic simplification, and model counting
must have separate timing boundaries and correctness oracles.

Preserve the lower bound: returning the complete truth vector of an arbitrary
function over `k` live variables requires `2^k` output bits. Learning may reduce
preparation, intermediate work, or exploit verified structure; it must not claim
to remove the cost of an explicitly requested complete result.

## 5. Trust and exactness boundary

The learned system is advisory. It may propose:

- a backend or ordered portfolio;
- a resource refusal;
- a motif boundary and functional label;
- a rewrite and substitution;
- a variable order, partition, or layout;
- initial SAT variable weights or polarities;
- a proof, rule, or prior-work retrieval candidate; or
- an abstention/fallback decision.

It may not establish logical truth, equivalence, soundness, successful training,
benchmark superiority, or artifact promotion by its own prediction.

Accepted results require an artifact-matched exact authority, for example:

- direct evaluation of a SAT assignment on the original expression;
- exhaustive truth tables for bounded small live support;
- an UNSAT miter checked independently for equivalence;
- an ROBDD/BDD identity check under an explicit manager/order contract;
- a theorem-listed rewrite instantiated with checked side conditions;
- GF(2) algebra for an admitted affine fragment;
- a proof certificate checked by an independent checker where supported; or
- differential agreement of independent exact implementations.

Every learned proposal must be rejectable without corrupting state or preventing
the deterministic fallback. If verification is missing, times out, exceeds a
budget, disagrees, or is uncertain, do not apply the proposal.

## 6. Minimum architecture

Use stable typed interfaces and keep the following modules separable:

1. **Canonical input adapters** for the existing Boolean AST/CM DAG first, with
   future CNF, AIG, ANF, and serialized-corpus adapters isolated.
2. **Task specification** containing output artifact, exactness, reuse count,
   latency/memory ceilings, cold/warm state, and allowed backends.
3. **Cheap deterministic feature extraction** that never performs the expensive
   computation it is intended to select.
4. **Exact recognizers** for structures that are directly and cheaply decidable.
5. **Optional learned proposers/rankers** behind versioned interfaces.
6. **Novelty and uncertainty policy** with an explicit abstention result.
7. **Bounded candidate-plan scheduler** with deterministic fallback and no
   automatic unbounded racing.
8. **Backend adapters** that preserve task-specific timing and result contracts.
9. **Independent result and transformation verification**.
10. **Content-aware local experiment records** stored only in approved product
    artifact roots, while the controller receives content-free hashes/counts.
11. **Dataset, run, model, and artifact manifests** with licenses, provenance,
    immutable hashes, split identities, seeds, environment, and resource use.
12. **Safe model serialization**. Do not load untrusted pickle, joblib, arbitrary
    Python, unsafe framework checkpoints, or executable model formats. Prefer
    bounded JSON schemas, NumPy arrays with strict shape/dtype checks, or a small
    generated decision program whose hash and evaluator are independently tested.
13. **Shadow/advisory integration**. A model candidate must first log what it would
    have selected while the deterministic baseline remains controlling.
14. **Rollback/bypass**. One configuration switch must disable all learned advice
    and restore the exact deterministic baseline without changing results.

Suggested data flow:

```text
input + requested query + resource/reuse contract
  -> canonical DAG and cheap invariant features
  -> exact detectors + learned proposals + novelty/confidence
  -> bounded candidate plan or deterministic fallback
  -> exact backend/transformation/search
  -> independent result/certificate/equivalence verification
  -> accepted result + decision trace + content-free controller evidence
```

## 12. Evaluation and promotion contract

### Always-required correctness

- zero accepted semantic mismatches;
- zero unverified applied rewrites or macro replacements;
- zero silent timeout, refusal, unsupported-backend, or missing-result rows;
- no output-budget or temporary-memory allocation after an admission refusal;
- deterministic fallback equivalence;
- model-disabled behavior equal to the deterministic baseline; and
- independent result checking outside primary timing windows, with verification
  overhead also reported in end-to-end totals.

### Router metrics

- virtual-best/oracle gap;
- total overhead-adjusted regret versus the best feasible backend;
- regret median, p90, p99, and maximum;
- catastrophic-choice counts at `>=2x`, timeout, and memory failure;
- prediction and feature-extraction overhead;
- feasibility precision/recall and calibration;
- abstention rate and fallback performance;
- cold/warm and query-count break-even surfaces; and
- cross-family and cross-machine generalization.

### Recognizer/rewrite metrics

- proposal precision and recall before exact verification;
- accepted false positives, which must remain zero;
- verification acceptance/rejection/timeout rates;
- downstream operation, node, depth, memory, and total-runtime change;
- recognition and verification overhead;
- benefit by motif and family; and
- negative-transfer and novel-family behavior.

### Search/order metrics

- total solve/build time, not only step count;
- decisions, conflicts, propagations, BDD nodes, reorder cost, or search-tree size
  as diagnostic metrics;
- model invocation count and time;
- best-of-`k` search cost and selected candidate rank; and
- proof/certificate validation where available.

### Owner-candidate promotion defaults

These are proposed defaults for review, not accepted execution thresholds:

1. Do not train a router unless the held-out virtual-best portfolio shows at least
   a 10% material end-to-end opportunity or a meaningful catastrophic tail beyond
   the best deterministic policy.
2. A router candidate should reduce family-held-out overhead-adjusted regret by at
   least 20% relative to the strongest non-learned baseline or demonstrate at
   least a 10% geometric-mean end-to-end improvement on the exact target task.
3. It must not increase timeout, memory-failure, or `>=2x` catastrophic choices.
4. Feature plus inference overhead should consume less than 10% of the predicted
   saving; otherwise use the fallback without model invocation.
5. A motif/rewrite candidate should show at least a 10% total improvement in its
   declared target stratum after recognition and verification overhead, with no
   accepted semantic errors and no unexplained p95 regression above 10%.
6. Promotion remains shadow-only until a second independently sourced held-out
   corpus and a second machine or explicitly deferred cross-machine decision are
   reviewed.

If the oracle ceiling is small, record the negative result and stop that learning
line. Do not optimize model accuracy when no end-to-end computational win is
possible.

### Statistical protocol

- Treat the formula, circuit, or independently generated family as the sampling
  unit. Timing rounds on one formula are repeated measurements, not independent
  samples.
- Use paired execution with randomized or alternating backend order, recorded
  warm/cold state, finite repetitions, and robust summaries.
- Report per-instance effects and family-clustered bootstrap intervals or another
  predeclared clustered uncertainty method.
- Tune only on the training/validation partitions. The held-out test partition
  remains sealed until the model, thresholds, feature set, and fallback policy are
  frozen.
- Run multiple training seeds when the learner is stochastic and report both
  central performance and variability.
- Separate scientific replication across machines from more rounds on one
  machine; do not pool raw timings across materially different platforms without
  a declared model.
